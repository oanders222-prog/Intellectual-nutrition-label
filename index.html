Know what’s really in your education.

Universities are facing a mental health crisis among students. The Intellectual Nutrition Label helps parents and students evaluate professors and the ideas they teach using publicly available information—so families can make informed decisions before paying tuition. Is your college or university class intellectually healthy, or does it promote anxiety, depression, and meaninglessness?
