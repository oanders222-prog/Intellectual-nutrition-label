About

The Intellectual Nutrition Label is a way to summarize the intellectual ingredients in a professor’s public work. It is designed to help parents and students understand the worldview, values, influences, and likely classroom emphases before enrolling in a course.

The goal of this project is to teach you the skills needed to evaluate any professor or class for yourself. You will learn to identify a professor's presuppositions about knowledge, reality, and value.
